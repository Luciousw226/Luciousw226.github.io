Prep Time | A Custom Payday 3 Menu Track

In my free time, I am an avid gamer and I have always enjoyed extensive customisation. Whether that be via weapons or characters, I will take every opportunity that I can to express myself via the protagonist of any game. Naturally, this extended into various modding scenes for games such as the Payday franchise.

When Payday 3 released, I found myself unhappy with the stock background music the game featured. A track named "Da Capo" is used for the main menu and personally, I found it to be ill-fitting for what the area calls for. Stood atop a roof, brooding with grungy green tones, presumably planning your next score, I took inspiration from the visuals onscreen and composed this track. Then, with the help of some associates, I was able to get it modded into the game and released to the public for use.

It has since gone on to be one of the most successful tracks posted to my YouTube channel and has received high praise such as "As far as I'm concerned, this is the Payday 3 official theme song. I've gotten too used to it." or "Coming back after months just to say: I've been using this track for so long, I honestly forgot that it's not the official default track. I would totally sign a petition to get Starbreeze to officially add this as the default main menu track!"
