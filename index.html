Who am I?

At first, I had little musical skill. All I had was a desire to sing. I wanted to do it professionally but I was young and I had no idea how I could actually develop a career doing this. I spent my early to mid teens interested in performing arts, video editing and film. It wasn't until I was a young adult that I landed on something I was really fixated on: music production.

I already had an interest in music software, using it to record vocals and lines for small projects with friends. I understood some basic musical principles and had years of using editing software casually. As a result I learned how to work through a DAW quickly and initially connected with Swedish production company DEP Films to help them with a simple track on one of their earlier short films. My early work with them was pretty minimal but it was through the continued opportunities they provided me and my own perseverance that I had the space to develop into the producer that I am now.

Today I continue to create scores for them and others with a sound that is distinctively me but will still fulfill your needs. I pride myself on defying expectations for what a type of score should sound like by focusing more on projects on an individual basis rather than what the requested genre of music might call for. I aim to always deliver something that is surprising to the client but still with a focus on working fast and meeting demands as I always have at every skill level.
